## 2. Coins earned per quiz, by score (they mirror the AL bands)

- a. **As proposed (rec):** AL1 (90%+) 8 coins plus 4 for a perfect score; AL2 7; AL3 6; AL4 5; AL5 4; AL6 3; AL7 2;
  AL8 1. Finishing always earns at least 1. WA/SA papers earn ×3.
- b. Flatter: everyone who finishes gets 5, plus 3 for 80% or more.
- c. Steeper: AL1 10, AL2–AL3 6, AL4–AL6 3, below that 1.

Small extras (all options): 1 per retest passed, 3 when the daily goal is met, 10 for a streak week.

## 3. Shop prices (the child picks the exact card; nothing is random)

- a. **As proposed (rec):** Common 30, Rare 60, Epic and Special 120. At 2 to 4 quizzes a day that is about one Epic a
  week.
- b. Cheaper (cards come faster): 20, 40, 80.
- c. Dearer (cards are rarer): 40, 80, 160.

## 4. Do tier clears still give a card as well as coins?

- a. **Yes (rec):** cards keep marking mastery; coins are extra.
- b. No: tier clears give coins only, and every card comes from the shop or an achievement.

## 5. The bar for the rare cards, which can't be bought

- a. **As proposed (rec):**
  - Super Epic: every sub-topic in a topic cleared at Competent (topic competency).
  - Legendary: a WA/SA paper at AL1–AL2.
  - Beast, Dragon, Witch and Ancient: the existing school milestones.
- b. Easier: Legendary also for any paper at AL3 or better.
- c. Harder: Legendary only for AL1, and Super Epic also needs an "Exam-ready" result in that topic's re-assessment.

## 6. When there is nothing left to buy

- a. **Coins buy stars for cards already owned (rec).**
- b. Coins buy decorations for My House (more to build).
- c. Both.
