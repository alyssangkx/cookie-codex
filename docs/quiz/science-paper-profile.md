# PSLE Science past-paper profile (counts only)

Summary of how school prelim and assessment papers (2018 to 2026) ask Science questions, used to plan the Science question bank. It contains counts and percentages only; no paper text, images, school names or paper identifiers. Counts come from automatic tagging that is about 84% accurate, so read them as close, not exact.

Sample: about 11,000 questions from nine years of papers. Marks share is each sub-topic's share of all printed marks.

## Overall shape

- MCQ is 66% of questions and 54% of marks (2 marks each, 4 options). Structured and open-ended parts are 34% of questions and 46% of marks, usually 1 to 4 marks per part.
- About 88% of questions carry a figure: apparatus or experiment set-ups, labelled diagrams, data tables, graphs and circuits are the main kinds.
- Open-ended parts are mostly "explain why or how" (about 70%), then state/name/identify, describe, and predict.
- MCQ option shapes: short phrases and single words about 60%, full statements about 24%, combinations such as "A and B only" about 8%.

## What this means for the bank

- The ten most tested sub-topics make up about 62% of marks; forces, energy conversion and food chains and webs alone are about 27%.
- The bank should be built most-tested first, with figure templates (tables, line graphs, circuits, food webs, set-ups, flow charts) generated by code, and drawn line art only for organisms and body systems.
- Open-ended parts are almost half the marks in the papers, so an MCQ-only bank covers about half of the exam style.
