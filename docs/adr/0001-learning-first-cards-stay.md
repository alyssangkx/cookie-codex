# Learning first, game mechanics second; awarded cards always stay; progress may be reset

Status: accepted. Date: 20 Nov 2026. Source: `docs/todo.md` (Priority), `docs/game-economy.md` (Context).

**Context.** The first question banks were a pilot and are being redesigned from the past-paper repository. Game rules (tiers, rewards, economy) could constrain that work.

**Decision.** The learning, training and assessment loop is the primary objective. Game mechanics are secondary and may be reworked. A card that was awarded is never taken away. Game progress (tier states, question states, attempts) may be wiped and reset when that simplifies a redesign.

**Consequences.** A redesign may move reward slots; owned cards stay owned and a duplicate becomes a star (or a replacement card, if the economy proposal is adopted). Progress resets are allowed but cards are never part of them.
