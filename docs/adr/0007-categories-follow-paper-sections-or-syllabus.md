# English and Chinese categories follow prelim paper sections; Math and Science follow SEAB sub-topics

Status: accepted. Date: 20 Nov 2026 (English reorganisation approved, in progress). Source: `docs/todo.md` (Schedule), `docs/learning-loop-design.md` (audit facts).

**Context.** English and Chinese prelim papers are organised by section type (for example Grammar MCQ, Comprehension Cloze), and tags by topic agreed poorly with sections (Chinese cloze tags matched the paper section 14% of the time). Math and Science papers are organised by syllabus topic.

**Decision.** English and Chinese categories follow the paper sections, so a child trains the format they will sit. English order: Grammar MCQ, Vocabulary MCQ, Vocabulary Cloze, Visual Text, Grammar Cloze, Editing, Comprehension Cloze, Synthesis & Transformation, Comprehension OE. Sections the site cannot ask yet show "Coming soon". Math and Science keep the SEAB syllabus sub-topics.

**Consequences.** Reward slots move when English categories change; owned cards stay (ADR 0001). Chinese sub-topics come from the section rule before they feed any diagnostic.
