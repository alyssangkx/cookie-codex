# PSLE Science 2027 syllabus survey (standard Science)

Retrieved 2026-11-17. Foundation Science (SEAB code 0039) exists as a separate paper with a reduced syllabus; it is not covered here.

## Which syllabus applies

The **2023 Primary Science Syllabus** (MOE, "Science Teaching & Learning Syllabus, Primary Three to Six", cover marked *Updated Jan 2026*) applies. The 2027 PSLE cohort started P3 in 2024, after the 2023 P3 implementation start.

> "PSLE SCIENCE For examination from 2026. ... The PSLE Science Paper assesses candidates' attainment in Science as stated in the 2023 primary science syllabus." (SEAB, 0009)

> "Implementation starting with 2023 Primary Three Cohort" (MOE syllabus cover)

Status: **unverified for 2027 specifically.** No SEAB document naming 2027 was found; "from 2026" has no end date, so 2027 applicability is an inference.

## Unverified

- **sci-cycles-repro-plants-3**: Outcome wording or level synthesised rather than quoted: fruit/seed dispersal adaptations are only cross-referenced in a Note under Interactions (P6); introduced in P5 Reproduction
- **sci-inquiry-evaluate-1**: Outcome wording or level synthesised rather than quoted: SEAB AO II(b) lists 'evaluating observations, information and methods'; the MOE syllabus has no explicit fair-test/variable-control outcome, so the fair-test wording is the compiler's reading
- **syllabus.appliesTo2027**: No SEAB document specifically for the 2027 PSLE was found. The only SEAB document found says 'For examination from 2026' with no end date and names the 2023 syllabus; 2027 applicability is inferred from that open-ended wording and the 2023 P3 implementation start.
- **MOE syllabus revision**: The MOE PDF (87 pp) says 'Updated Jan 2026' and implementation from the 2023 P3 cohort. Earlier versions (Oct 2022, May 2024) were reported by search snippets but not retrieved, so what changed in Jan 2026 is unknown.
- **Underlined topics (Foundation exemptions)**: Table 2 says underlined topics are not required for Foundation Science, but underlining is lost in text extraction. Standard-only tags here are inferred from topics/outcomes missing on the Foundation learning-outcome pages (e.g. Energy Conversion has no Foundation page).
- **psleExaminable by level**: SEAB states the paper assesses the 2023 primary science syllabus (P3-P6) and does not split P3/P4 from P5/P6 content; all outcomes are therefore tagged examinable. The syllabus is spiral, so P3/P4 content is assumed testable at P6.
- **Paper weighting by assessment objective**: SEAB lists AO I (Knowledge with Understanding) and AO II (Application of Knowledge and Scientific Inquiry) but gives no percentage weightings.
- **Calculator policy**: The SEAB format page does not mention calculators; recorded as false (not stated).
- **mcqTestable / needsDiagram tags**: These are the compiler's judgement, not stated by the syllabus.
- **Foundation Science**: Foundation Science (SEAB code 0039) exists as a separate paper with a reduced syllabus; out of scope here.
- **Level of inquiry-skill outcomes**: The syllabus has no standalone skills strand; skills sit in the 'Practices' column per topic. Level tag P3 means first appearing at P3 and spiralling to P6.
