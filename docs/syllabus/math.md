# PSLE Mathematics (Standard) syllabus survey, 2027 PSLE

Surveyed 2026-11-17. Machine-readable version: `site/src/data/syllabus/math.json` (13 topics, 181 outcomes).

## Which syllabus applies

The cohort sitting PSLE in 2027 (P6 in 2027) is on the **MOE 2021 Primary Mathematics Syllabus** (implementation started with the 2021 Primary One cohort; document updated Oct 2025) and the SEAB **PSLE Mathematics (0008), "For Examination from 2026"** document. 2026 was the first PSLE under the 2021 syllabus.

Evidence, MOE syllabus footnote (pp. 30-31), quoted exactly:

> "In 2025, the 2021 primary mathematics syllabus applies to Primary 1 to Primary 5 only. Students in Primary 6 will continue to use the 2013 syllabus. The 2021 mathematics syllabus will be applicable to Primary 6 from 2026 onwards."

Caveat: SEAB has not published a "2027" Mathematics document that I could find. The newest is "For Examination from 2026", which has no end date, so it is treated as current for 2027. See the unverified list.

Foundation Mathematics (P5-P6 Foundation, SEAB 0038) also exists; it is not covered here.

| 1 | A | Multiple-choice | 8 | 2 | 16 | | No |
| 1 | B | Short-answer | 12 | 2 | 24 | | No |
| 2 | one booklet | Short-answer | 5 | 2 | 10 | 1 h 20 min | Yes |
| 2 | one booklet | Structured / long-answer | 10 | 3, 4 or 5 | 40 | | Yes |
| Total | | | 45 | | 100 | 2 h 30 min | |

MCQ has four options, one correct. The 1-mark MCQs "assess basic concepts and skills". Both papers are on the same day with a break. Short-answer: 1 mark per part on two-part questions; on one-part questions a wrong answer can still earn 1 mark for correct method.

## Unverified

- No SEAB 2027 syllabus document exists on seab.gov.sg as of 2026-11-17; applicability to 2027 is inferred from the MOE sentence above and the open-ended SEAB 2026 document.
- Paper format is from the 2026 document; no 2027-specific statement found.
- `psleExaminable` is true for every Standard outcome because SEAB gives no per-outcome list; P1-P3 content is assessed implicitly as prerequisite.
- `mcqTestable` and `needsDiagram` are author judgement, not official.
- One P6 volume line ("use of ..., 3") lost its symbols in PDF extraction; recorded as an unverified placeholder (`math-area-and-volume-volume-of-cube-and-cuboid-*`), not for quizzing.
