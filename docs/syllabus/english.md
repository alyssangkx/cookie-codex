# PSLE English Language 2027 syllabus survey

## Which syllabus applies

PSLE English Language (Standard) is assessed against the MOE English Language Teaching and Learning Syllabus 2020 (Primary). The SEAB document is headed "Implemented from the Year of Examination 2025" and states:

> "The purpose of the examination is to assess the candidates' attainment in English Language based on the Learning Outcomes stated in the English Language Teaching and Learning Syllabus 2020 (Primary)."

The MOE document is headed "Implementation starting with 2020 Primary One Cohort", so the 2027 cohort (Primary One in 2022) is on it. **Unverified:** no source names PSLE 2027 explicitly. SEAB has not published a 2027 formats page or syllabus, and no newer primary EL syllabus was found. Applicability to 2027 is inferred. Foundation English is a separate syllabus, not covered here.

## Paper format (200 marks)

| Paper | Weighting | Duration | Items | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Writing | 25% | 1 h 10 min | Situational Writing (1, OE, 14); Continuous Writing (1, OE, 36) | 50 |
| 2 Language Use and Comprehension | 45% | 1 h 50 min | Booklet A MCQ: Grammar 10, Vocabulary 5, Vocabulary Cloze 5, Visual Text Comprehension 5. Booklet B OE: Grammar Cloze 10, Editing 10, Comprehension Cloze 15, Synthesis/Transformation 5 (2 marks each), Comprehension OE 10 (2 marks each) | 90 |
| 3 Listening Comprehension | 10% | about 35 min | 20 MCQ | 20 |
| 4 Oral Communication | 20% | about 10 min (5 min prep) | Reading Aloud 15; Stimulus-based Conversation (photo) 25 | 40 |

MCQ-assessed marks: 25 (Paper 2 Booklet A) + 20 (Paper 3) = 45 of 200 (22.5%).

## Non-MCQ components

| Component | Marks | MCQ conversion note |
|---|---|---|
| Situational Writing (Paper 1) | 14 | Open-ended functional writing; not convertible, though 'choose the best opening or tone for this email' MCQs could rehearse purpose/audience/register. |
| Continuous Writing (Paper 1) | 36 | Composition from pictures; not convertible; only idea-selection or sentence-improvement MCQs could support. |
| Grammar Cloze (Paper 2 Booklet B) | 10 | Open-ended single-word fill; convertible to 4-option grammar MCQ practice. |
| Editing for Spelling and Grammar (Paper 2 Booklet B) | 10 | Convertible to 'which underlined part is wrong' MCQs. |
| Comprehension Cloze (Paper 2 Booklet B) | 15 | Convertible to MCQ cloze practice. |
| Synthesis / Transformation (Paper 2 Booklet B) | 10 | Partly convertible: choose the best combined or transformed sentence from 4 options. |
| Comprehension OE (Paper 2 Booklet B) | 20 | Written answers; could be practised as MCQ literal/inferential questions on short passages, not a true equivalent. |
| Reading Aloud (Paper 4) | 15 | Spoken; not convertible. |
| Stimulus-based Conversation (Paper 4) | 25 | Spoken; not convertible. |

## Unverified

- **Syllabus/format applicability to PSLE 2027**: No SEAB 'PSLE Formats Examined in 2027' page or 2027 PSLE English syllabus PDF was found (2026 page last updated 24 April 2026; the 2027 URL returns 404). The most recent SEAB document says 'Implemented from the Year of Examination 2025' with no end date, and no replacement primary EL syllabus was found; 2027 applicability is therefore inferred, not stated.
- **Specific testable grammar items (tenses, subject-verb agreement, prepositions, connectors, pronouns, question tags)**: Specific grammar items are not named in the SEAB PSLE English syllabus or the MOE EL Syllabus 2020 (Primary) text; the Grammar 'Items and Structures' detail is not in the published PDF text. Listed as unverified placeholders only.
- **Paper 2 Booklet A and B separate durations**: SEAB gives one combined duration of 1 h 50 min for Paper 2; booklet split is not stated.
- **Oral duration and Paper 3 duration**: SEAB states 'About 10 min' and 'About 35 min', so durationMin is approximate.
- **Foundation English**: Exists (separate SEAB syllabus 0031 and MOE Foundation syllabus); not surveyed here, out of scope.
